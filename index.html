THE QUICK GUIDE

How to Play

Keep the ball in play. Outsmart the computer. Have fun.

  1. Move your paddle. Use your mouse or ↑ ↓ / W S keys. On a phone, drag your finger across the arena.
  2. Hit the ball back. Aim with your paddle and don't let the ball pass you.
  3. Score to win. Reach 7 points and lead by at least 2; at 15 points, the leader wins.
  4. Choose your pace and level. Levels controls AI difficulty. Progression unlocks the next level when you win; practice accepts any level without unlocking or progression wins. Chill is relaxed, Classic is balanced, and Turbo is fast.
  5. Take a break. Press Pause (or P / Escape) and choose Resume for a 5-second countdown.
  6. Earn Fireball. Return about five shots to charge. You have 8 seconds to tap Fire or press Space, then make your next return within that same window. The boosted shot has a short flame trail and normal hitbox. The computer earns the same ability. Disable Arcade Fireball in Settings for pure rally.

Tip: Start with Chill while learning. You can turn sounds on or off using ♫.
